Do the shows express manosphere ideas, or only use the words?

The other tabs count words. A word count cannot tell whether a speaker is endorsing an idea, mocking it, criticizing it, or using the same word for something unrelated ("body count" also means war casualties). In this pilot, a language model read short transcript excerpts one at a time and judged whether the speakers express any of eleven ideas that define the manosphere in academic research.

Scope: 19 manosphere shows, episodes released August 5 to November 4, 2024. 476 excerpts coded: a random sample of 152 and every one of the 324 excerpts that contain a LISTN or Krendel term. These are early, single-coder results with no human check yet.

Coding: Claude Sonnet 5.5, run in-session in 19 batches with one fixed prompt (codebook v1). Every quoted passage was checked automatically against the transcript; 0 mismatches. Transcripts are automated captions, so wording is approximate and some profanity is censored as “[ __ ]”. Full write-up: 2026-11-01 LLM Ideology Pilot Report.
